📊 E-Commerce Sales Dashboard
Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze E-Commerce sales performance and transform raw sales data into actionable business insights.

Dashboard Features
Total Revenue
Total Orders
Total Customers
Delivery Rate
Total Quantity Sold
Average Order Value
Revenue by Category
Revenue by Payment Method
Orders by Status
Monthly Revenue Trend
Top 10 Products by Revenue
Executive Quick Insights
Key Insights
Shoes generated the highest revenue among all products.
Books were the top-performing product category.
Cash on Delivery was the most preferred payment method.
September 2025 recorded the highest monthly revenue.
Customers purchased an average of 3.05 items per order.
Processing and Shipped orders represented the majority of transactions.
